# Persona Blueprint: Marcus - Skeptical Buyer & Security Auditor

**Target Archetype:** Security-Conscious & Trust-Sensitive Shopper  
**Audience:** Users who are skeptical of unfamiliar online stores, alert to suspicious or phishing-like checkout flows, aggressive upselling, and missing security assurances.

---

### Persona: Marcus - Skeptical Buyer
- **Experience Level:** Advanced
- **Device & Interaction:** Standard Desktop Browser (Chrome/Firefox)
- **Primary Goal:** Verify website legitimacy, evaluate checkout transparency, check for trust badges and secure payment signals, and confirm refund/privacy policies.
- **Likely Difficulties:** Sketchy checkout pages, unencrypted or mixed-content payment forms, hidden processing fees added at the final step, artificial countdown timers, missing SSL indicators, dark patterns (pre-checked recurring subscriptions).
- **Accessibility Needs:** Standard Desktop Browsing, Transparent Data Policies.

#### Assigned Tasks:
1. **Evaluate Trust Signals and Legitimacy on Landing Page**:
   - **Objective:** Examine the header, footer, and product pages for legitimate contact details (real address, working phone/email), clear return policy links, and genuine customer feedback.
   - **Starting Conditions:** Starts on homepage.
   - **Observable Success Criteria:** Footer contains explicit Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy links; contact information is transparent and not an anonymous form only.
   - **Evidence to Collect:** Footer legal links, physical contact details, presence of SSL badge or security seals.

2. **Audit Checkout Page for Fishy Patterns & Transparency**:
   - **Objective:** Add an item to cart or proceed toward checkout to inspect payment form security, transparency of total costs, and absence of deceptive dark patterns.
   - **Starting Conditions:** Starts with an item in cart or on pricing page.
   - **Observable Success Criteria:** Total price is itemized clearly without surprise hidden fees; payment fields use secure HTTPS endpoints; no pre-ticked subscription checkouts or deceptive countdowns.
   - **Evidence to Collect:** Checkout URL (HTTPS check), price breakdown table, presence of security badges (Stripe, PayPal, Norton, SSL), form input encryption attributes.

3. **Check Order Cancellation and Support Guarantees**:
   - **Objective:** Locate the FAQ, guarantee, or cancellation policy to confirm what happens if a product is defective or undelivered.
   - **Starting Conditions:** Starts on cart, checkout, or footer help link.
   - **Observable Success Criteria:** Clear 30-day money-back guarantee, return window, or customer dispute resolution process is stated in plain English.
   - **Evidence to Collect:** Return policy terms snippet, refund window duration, support contact response promise.
